## 3. Product Card Dual-View Architecture

Each product card integrates:
1. **Interactive Dual-View Switcher:** A clean floating segmented control with `[Product]` and `[👤 On Model]` buttons directly on the photo.
2. **Desktop Hover Transition:** Moving the cursor over the card smoothly previews the model wearing the piece.
3. **Picture Reference Label:** A dedicated badge under the photo displays the exact filename (`P06_..._Main_Preview.jpg` or `P06_..._Lifestyle_PREVIEW.jpg`).
4. **Quick View Gallery:** The modal provides side-by-side thumbnails and full-size toggle for Product View, On Model View, and Macro Closeup.
